Welcome!

815Creatives is the home for the creative community whether professional, hobbyist, or just interested in learning more about the creative arts.
Our mission is to foster creativity and innovation in the Rockford area by providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all creatives to thrive.

Local Mural Project Receives City Grant

Rockford Arts Journal

This civic grant will fund the next phase of a community-led mural, giving artists and neighborhood volunteers resources to expand the project. The design will celebrate local history while creating a vibrant public gathering point. Organizers say the new funding also includes art education workshops for nearby schools.

New Collaborative Studio Opens Downtown

815Creatives Dispatch

A shared creative workspace has opened in the heart of Rockford, offering makers, writers, and digital artists a place to work together. Members can access equipment, mentorship, and weekly critique sessions. The studio is already hosting its first community showcase this month.

May 28, 2026

Upcoming Summer Workshops Announced

Rockford Creative Weekly

815Creatives has released its summer schedule of workshops spanning writing, glassblowing, film, audio production, and digital art. Registration opens soon, with early-bird pricing for local residents. The series aims to connect emerging artists with experienced instructors.

Podcast Series Features District Artists

Midwest Arts Review

A new podcast series highlights the stories and inspirations behind Rockford's most exciting creative talents. Each episode blends interviews, audio art, and behind-the-scenes looks at collaborative projects. Listeners can expect fresh perspectives on community building and creative practice.
