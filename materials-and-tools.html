Materials and Tools

Explore the materials and tools used by our creative community.



Field Notebook & Fountain Pen

For drafting short stories and capturing morning observations, a reliable notebook and a smooth-filling fountain pen help me keep ideas flowing. I prefer a dot-grid Moleskine and a medium-nib pen for quick sketches of scenes.

Creative: Anya Sharma

Portable Recorder & Spoken Word Prep

Recording live performances and field interviews is central to my process; a compact recorder and lapel mic capture the immediacy of urban rhythms that inform my poetry and pieces of narrative journalism.

Creative: Benjamin Carter

Sketchbook, Wacom & Reference Scans

Combining long-form reporting with illustrated sequences requires both a sketchbook for thumbnails and a Wacom tablet for digital inking. I also scan archival materials to support factual storytelling in graphic form.

Creative: David Chen


Porcelain Clay & Pinch Tools

I work primarily with porcelain clay and a small set of hand tools. A reliable kiln profile and test tiles for glazes are essential when developing new surface treatments for sculpture and functional ware.

Creative: Maria Lorenzo

Forge, Anvil & High-Carbon Steel

Blacksmithing demands a well-maintained forge, a solid anvil, and quality high-carbon steel stock. Proper tongs and finishing files make the difference between a piece that holds and one that falters.

Creative: Sam Osei

Glassblowing Furnace & Annealing Oven

Working with molten glass requires a reliable furnace, glass rods and a well-calibrated annealing oven to relieve stress. Borosilicate glass and proper protective gear are non-negotiable for safety and clarity of color.

Creative: Chloe Jones


Memory Cards

Memory cards are essential for storing and transferring video footage. They come in various formats, such as SD, microSD, and CFexpress, each offering different storage capacities and speeds. Choosing the right memory card depends on the camera's requirements and the type of video being recorded.

My preferred memory card is the SanDisk Extreme Pro SDXC UHS-I card, as it offers fast read and write speeds, making it ideal for capturing high-quality video footage without any jitters.

Creative: Alex Dubois

Primes, Stabilizer & ND Filters

For experimental cinematography I rely on a set of prime lenses, a compact gimbal for fluid movement, and a selection of neutral density filters to control exposure without altering depth of field. These tools let me capture deliberate, meditative imagery.

Creative: Kenji Tanaka

Field Recorder & Binaural Mics

My work depends on capturing layered environmental sound; a field recorder with interchangeable microphones and a binaural rig helps create immersive podcast episodes and experimental soundscapes.

Creative: Latoya Williams

Condenser Microphone & Home Booth

For voice acting and narrative work, a high-quality condenser mic, pop filter, and a treated home booth provide the clarity and warmth needed for character-driven audio performances.

Creative: Katrina Cartwright

DAW, MIDI Controller & Atmos Setup

Producing spatial music requires a robust digital audio workstation, a MIDI controller for hands-on composition, and access to a Dolby Atmos-capable monitoring setup to mix in immersive formats.

Creative: Morris James

Generative Libraries & Touch Interfaces

I build interactive pieces using creative-coding libraries like p5.js and TouchDesigner, paired with touch-enabled displays to allow audiences to influence generative systems in real time.

Creative: Casey Miller

GPU Servers & Visualization Tooling

Large-scale generative visuals rely on GPU-accelerated servers, Python toolchains, and libraries like D3 and WebGL to transform datasets into evolving art pieces displayed both online and in public installations.

Creative: Marcus Thompson
