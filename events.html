Events

Join us for exciting events and workshops that provide the opportunity to unleash your creativity and talent.



Pop-up Studio Series

A rotating set of free, drop-in studio sessions where creatives can experiment with materials, tools, and collaborative techniques.

Date: Every Saturday, 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Location: Rockford Public Library Main - Maker Lab

Age Group: All ages

Cost: Free

Rockford Filmmaker Showcase

An evening celebration of independent films created by local and regional filmmakers, featuring screenings, Q&A sessions, and networking opportunities.

Date: Third Friday of each month, 6:30 PM - 9:00 PM

Location: The Coronado Theater

Age Group: 16+

Cost: $10 general admission

Creative Writing Workshop

Learn storytelling techniques from experienced writers through hands-on workshops covering fiction, poetry, and creative nonfiction.

Date: Wednesdays, 7:00 PM - 8:30 PM (6-week sessions)

Location: Rockford Arts Guild - Studio A

Age Group: 18+

Cost: $75 per 6-week session

Open Mic Night for Performers

A welcoming community space for poets, musicians, comedians, and storytellers to share their work and connect with fellow artists.

Date: Every second Thursday, 7:00 PM - 9:30 PM

Location: Brew Haven Coffee House

Age Group: All ages (non-alcoholic drinks available)

Cost: Free (optional $5 donation)

Digital Art & Coding Bootcamp

Intensive workshop exploring creative coding, generative art, and interactive design tools for digital creators of all skill levels.

Date: July 15-19, 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Location: Rockford Innovation Hub

Age Group: 16+

Cost: $150 per participant

Community Mural Project

Join fellow visual artists in creating a large-scale collaborative mural celebrating Rockford's creative spirit and cultural diversity.

Date: August 5 - August 12 (daily, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM)

Location: Downtown Rockford (Main Street corner)

Age Group: All ages (minors need adult supervision)

Cost: Free

Podcast Production Masterclass

Learn the complete process of producing a podcast from concept through post-production with industry professionals guiding the way.

Date: Saturdays, June 14 - July 12, 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Location: Rockford Community Radio Station

Age Group: 18+

Cost: $120 for full course

Artist Talk & Gallery Reception

Evening gathering featuring presentations from featured 815Creatives artists, followed by refreshments and informal gallery viewing of their works.

Date: First Saturday of month, 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM

Location: Rockford Arts Guild Main Gallery

Age Group: All ages

Cost: Free
