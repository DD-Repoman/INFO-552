A little about me


Background

I am a library lover who came to the field in an unconventional manner. My background is actually in finance. My bachelor's degree is in Accounting and I also received a minor in economics. After graduation I did a brief stint at an auditing firm before ending up in municipal finance at the City of Rockford. I gradually rose from Accountant to Billing and Payments Analyst and finally Customer Service Manager of the payment center over about 10 years before moving to the Finance Director of my local public library.

During the next 10 years of my career I found myself pulled more and more toward the mission of public libraries and the mentorship of a wonderful Executive Director. I was able to advocate for and oversee our Maker Lab, a public service area! I advocated for and built a case study demonstrating for the need to return to mobile library services which had ended sometime around the 1970s. We now have three mobile library vehicles in our fleet! I found a way to fund our first cohort of adult online high school students and designed and delivered presentations to a local businessman who valued education and would go on to not only fund our program with generous donations, but would also go on to bring friends and acquaintances to events where the library shared results and grew a donor-funded program that allows us to address educational attainment in our community. Now I find myself the Executive Director of my public library working diligently to connect people with information as well as provide people the same wonderful opportunities for growth that were provided to me.
