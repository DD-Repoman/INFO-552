About 815Creatives

815Creatives is a vibrant community of artists, makers, and creatives based in Rockford, IL.

We believe in the power of creativity to inspire change and foster a sense of community.

Whether you're an artist, designer, writer, or maker, 815Creatives is here to help you thrive and connect
with others who share your passion.

Join us as we celebrate creativity and build a stronger, more connected creative community in Rockford!
