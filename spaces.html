Spaces

Below is a list of creative spaces in the Rockford area that are open to the public, including makerspaces, galleries, and performance venues. These spaces provide resources, inspiration, and opportunities for collaboration for creatives of all disciplines.



Rockford Makerspace

A community workshop equipped with tools for woodworking, metalworking, 3D printing, and electronics. Offers classes and open studio hours for members.

Location: 123 Main St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Mon-Fri 10am-8pm, Sat 10am-6pm

Cost: $50/month membership or $10/day pass

Rockford Public Library Maker Lab

A community workshop equipped with podcasting studio, green screen, and editing facilities, laser cutter, 3D-printers, sewing machines, serger, embroidery machine, direct to film printer, Cricut cutting machine, and heat press.

Location: 215 N Wyman St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Mon-Fri 10am-8pm, Sat 12pm-5pm

Cost: Free, provided materials have nominal costs

Artspace Gallery

A contemporary art gallery showcasing work by local and regional artists. Hosts exhibitions, artist talks, and workshops.

Location: 456 Elm St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Wed-Sun 12pm-6pm

Cost: Free admission

The Creative Hub

A collaborative workspace for writers, musicians, and digital artists. Provides private studios, shared resources, and regular networking events.

Location: 789 Oak St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Mon-Fri 9am-9pm, Sat-Sun 10am-6pm

Cost: $100/month membership or $15/day pass

Riverfront Studio Collective

An open-access studio space for painters, printmakers, and sculptors that also hosts monthly community crit sessions and pop-up exhibitions.

Location: 210 River St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Tue-Sat 11am-7pm

Cost: $35/day studio pass or $120/month studio membership

Echo Sound Lab

A creative audio production space with recording booths, mixing workstations, and live rehearsal rooms designed for musicians, podcasters, and sound designers.

Location: 332 Walnut Ave, Rockford, IL

Hours: Mon-Fri 10am-10pm, Sat 12pm-8pm

Cost: $25/hour studio time or $175/session package

Bright Pixel Lab

A digital media lab offering high-performance workstations, AR/VR gear, and collaborative design space for creative coders and UX designers.

Location: 95 Bridge St, Rockford, IL

Hours: Mon-Thu 9am-8pm, Fri 9am-5pm

Cost: $60/day pass or $180/month membership

Studio 815 Performance Loft

A performance venue and rehearsal loft that supports theater makers, spoken-word artists, and dance ensembles with affordable rental rates and technical support.

Location: 148 Market St, Rockford, IL

Hours: By appointment, with weekend event access

Cost: $45/hour rehearsal rate or $250/day performance rental
