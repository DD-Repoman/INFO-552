Professional Associations


Illinois Library Association (ILA) is a statewide organization that provides services, training, discounts, conferences and other opportunities to libraries in Illinois.

American Library Association (ALA) is a nationwide organization that provides services, training, discounts, conferences and other opportunities to libraries in the US.

Public Library Association (PLA) is a professional organization for those who work with public libraries. PLA is a sub-organization of ALA and provides many of the same services, just geared specifically at public libraries.

Rotary International is an organization that promotes health, education, peace, access to life essentials, and growing communities. Many of the very same interests of the public library and goals that can be achieved by connecting people to information.
